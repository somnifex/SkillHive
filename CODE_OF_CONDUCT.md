# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SkillHive should be a place where people can collaborate without harassment or exclusion. We
welcome participants of every background, identity, experience level, and point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Make space for others and acknowledge their contributions.
- Protect private information and follow responsible security disclosure.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to keep the community safe and productive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained
disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.
Abusive behavior outside project spaces may also be considered when it affects community safety.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community events,
and when someone publicly represents SkillHive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. The repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/somnifex/SkillHive/security/advisories/new) can be used
for a confidential report when enabled; do not place sensitive conduct reports in public issues.
Maintainers will limit disclosure to the people needed to review the report, avoid conflicts of
interest, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants according to
severity and history.

Good-faith reports and appeals will be reviewed fairly. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a
violation.
